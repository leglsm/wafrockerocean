Capacity assumptions (by line)

WAF

Per row: Needed = Daily max ÷ slots/Section; Proposal = ceil(Needed). Capacity vs owned uses Σ(4 × Proposal) on WAF (four sub-parts per color).

Rocker

Rocker: Proposal rounds racks needed up to the next 0.5 (e.g. 1.24→1.5). Capacity vs owned uses Σ(2 × Proposal). Current column is ignored.
